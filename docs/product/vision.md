# Product vision

> Status: draft. Expand as requirements are confirmed.

## Problem

Certification preparation is fragmented. Learners move between question banks, videos, documentation, forums, and their own notes, with no single view of what they know and what they don't. Preparation is often reduced to memorizing question banks, which can help someone pass an exam without understanding the material.

## Vision

ExamOps helps people prepare for certifications properly. It helps them:

- **Practice** with realistic questions and mock exams.
- **Understand their mistakes** through clear explanations.
- **Learn the concepts** behind each question, not just the answers.
- **Track their progress** across the topics an exam covers.
- **Know when they are ready** to take the real exam.

## Initial audience

People preparing for technical certifications.

## Initial content

- Original practice questions
- Explanations for each question
- Study notes
- Mock exams

## Content integrity

ExamOps will not depend on copied proprietary exam questions or brain dumps. All content is original and is reviewed by a person before it is published.

## Future possibilities

- AI-assisted learning, such as personalized study plans and on-demand explanations. AI-generated content is always reviewed by a person before it is published.
- Hands-on labs for practical skills.

These are directions, not commitments. Each will be scoped and requested explicitly before it is built.
